Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started?
Install the app, register with your name, phone, email, a 4-digit PIN and a profile photo, fund your wallet and pick a plan.
How do I fund my wallet?
Your dashboard shows your personal virtual account number. Transfer to it from any bank app and your wallet balance updates.
Is my money safe?
Every purchase and withdrawal needs your 4-digit PIN, which is verified on our secure servers. Too many wrong attempts lock the PIN temporarily.
Why is my account linked to one device?
It protects you from account sharing and theft. If you change phones, contact support and we will authorise the new device.
Can I become an agent or reseller?
Yes. Partners get a reseller dashboard and can withdraw earnings to a bank account (a 1% network fee applies). Call or message us to get started.
Which devices are supported?
Android phones and tablets, iPhone and iPad, and Windows computers. You can also install the web app from your browser.
